Privacy Policy for FisCal Effective Date: 06/05/2026 At FisCal, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application. 1. Permissions and Why They Are Needed FisCal requests several permissions to provide its core features. Here is why we need them: • Camera: Used to scan barcodes on food packaging. This allows the app to quickly identify products and retrieve nutritional information. • Health Connect (Read/Write): Used to sync your nutritional intake, weight, and height with your device's central health dashboard. This ensures your fitness data is consistent across all your health apps. • Internet Access: Used to communicate with our food database (to fetch product info) and to sync crowdsourced price data. • Notifications & Alarms: Used to send you meal reminders and track your nutrition goals throughout the day. • Post-Notifications: Required on Android 13+ to show you alerts for meal logging. 2. What Data We Collect and Where It Goes We differentiate between your Personal Health Data and Community Data. Personal Health Data • What it is: Your weight, height, personal meal logs, and nutritional intake. • Where it stays: This data is stored locally on your device within the FisCal app database. • Health Connect: If you grant permission, this data is synced with Google Health Connect. This data is stored in your Google Account and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. FisCal does not upload your personal meal history or body measurements to our own private servers. Community Data (Crowdsourced) • What it is: Information about food products (names, brands, nutritional facts per 100g) and their prices at specific shops. • Where it goes: When you add a new product or update a price, this information is uploaded to our Supabase Cloud Database. • Anonymity: This data is submitted anonymously. We do not link these submissions to your personal identity or your private health logs. 3. Data Storage and Security • Local Storage: We use Android's Room database and DataStore to keep your personal logs secure on your phone. • Online Storage: Community food data is stored in a secure Supabase instance. We do not store any personally identifiable information (PII) like names, email addresses, or phone numbers in this database. 4. Who Has Access to Your Data? • You: You have full control over your local data and can delete your meal history at any time within the app. • The Community: Other FisCal users can see the food products, nutritional info, and prices that you contribute to the crowdsourced database, but they cannot see who contributed them. • Third-Party Services: ◦ Open Food Facts: When scanning a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product data. ◦ Google Health Connect: If enabled, Google manages the visibility of your health data to other apps based on your system settings. 5. Your Choices You can revoke any permission (Camera, Health Connect, etc.) at any time through your Android System Settings. Note that certain features, like barcode scanning or health syncing, will stop working if these permissions are revoked. 6.
